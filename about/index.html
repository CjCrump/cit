About

I'm Chance.

I swapped my first GPU at 14 and never stopped taking things apart. Computers, servers, whatever someone would let me open up.

By day I'm a journeyman laborer. That matters more than you'd think. I know what it costs you when a job goes sideways, a tool quits, or a workday gets wasted on the phone with somebody who won't give you a straight answer.

So that's how I run this. One guy, one number, a flat price before I start, and I finish what I start.
